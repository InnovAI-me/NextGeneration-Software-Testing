\subsection{Review Protocol}
This review follows the systematic literature review (SLR) guidelines
of Kitchenham and Charters~\cite{kitchenham2007} and reports study
selection using the PRISMA 2020 flow diagram~\cite{page2021prisma}.
The complete, machine-readable protocol (search strings, databases,
inclusion/exclusion criteria, and quality assessment checklist) is
versioned in \texttt{literature-review/} of the accompanying
repository for full reproducibility.

\subsection{Search Strategy}
A curated starter corpus of 113 candidate studies was assembled from a
hand-curated reference list spanning 19 technique categories (see
\texttt{literature-review/reference-set-provenance.md}) to bootstrap
screening and taxonomy design. The full systematic multi-database
search (IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Scopus, Web of Science, plus
arXiv/Google Scholar for preprints, with one round of backward/forward
snowballing) defined in
\texttt{literature-review/search-strategy.md} is planned but not yet
executed/logged (see \texttt{docs/research-plan.md}, Phase 1); results
from that search will be merged with and deduplicated against the
current 113-study corpus before final inclusion counts are reported
here.

\subsection{Study Selection Process}
\textit{[Describe the PRISMA stages: identification, screening,
eligibility, inclusion. Reference the PRISMA diagram generated from
\texttt{literature-review/prisma-flow.md}.]} Of the 113 candidate
studies, 31 were prioritized as ``must-read'' primary studies (based on
peer-reviewed venue, direct relevance to agentic/autonomous testing,
and category coverage) and analyzed in full text; full-text or
verified-abstract coverage currently stands at 102 of 113 studies (99
full text, 3 abstract-only), with 11 studies pending manual
institutional-access retrieval (paywalled/blocked sources). See
\texttt{data/papers/fetch-log.csv} for the complete retrieval manifest.
